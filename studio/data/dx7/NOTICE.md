# DX7 patch data

`dx7-catalog-sample.json`, `dx7-rom-catalog.json` and `dx7-stock-banks.json` were downloaded from
https://rubato.audio/ (the Plaits Palette bank composer) by `tools/dx7_composer/fetch_palette_library.py`
on 2026-10-12.

Origin, as stated in the `provenance` block of `dx7-rom-catalog.json`: Yamaha DX7 factory ROM cartridges,
Yamaha VRC voice ROM expansion cartridges, and the third-party GreyMatter Response E! soundbanks, obtained by
Rubato Audio from https://yamahablackboxes.com/collection/yamaha-dx7-synthesizer/patches/ (2026-08-04).

Rights, quoted in substance: the voice parameter data originates with its rights holders, Yamaha Corporation for the
factory and VRC cartridges and GreyMatter Response for the E! soundbanks. Rubato Audio claims no ownership of and
grants no licence over this data. No firmware or ROM image is included. Rubato Audio is not affiliated with Yamaha or
GreyMatter Response.
